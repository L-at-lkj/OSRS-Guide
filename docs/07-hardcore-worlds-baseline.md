## Hardcore Worlds (game mode)
- **Launched 4 Nov 2026** (11:00 UTC per one report). A members' mode on separate servers; one life per character. A hotfix on 16 Nov reportedly added an F2P Hardcore world. *(Leads: wiki "Hardcore Worlds", Jagex press release, MassivelyOP 5 Nov 2026)*
- **Death rules = the Hardcore Group Ironman ruleset**, which Jagex calls "much less forgiving". Expect about 99% of deaths to count.
- **Reported UNSAFE on Hardcore Worlds although usually safe for HCIM** (per the wiki's copy of Jagex's announcement): Barbarian Assault, Camelot Training Room, Chambers of Xeric, Dream World during Lunar Diplomacy, Fishing Trawler, Inferno, Magic Training Arena, Nightmare Zone, Pest Control, **Random Events**, **Rogues' Den**, TzHaar Fight Cave, **Player-owned house**. One guide also lists the PvM Arena. ⚠ Sources differ on the edges.
- **Reported SAFE exceptions:** Castle Wars, Clan Wars, Soul Wars, TzHaar Fight Pit, the 4th phase of the Fremennik Trials, and the Karamja Elite diary resurrection reward. Some guides add the Western Provinces Elite resurrection. ⚠ To be verified.
- **On an unsafe death:** the character goes to the Reaper. Choose **Reset** (unlimited, fresh run) or **Transfer** (permanent move to the main game).
- **Economy:** trading and a Grand Exchange **separate from the main game**; items stay within Hardcore Worlds.
- **Hiscores:** Hardcore Worlds has its own board, `https://secure.runescape.com/m=hiscore_oldschool_hardcore_mains/` (confirmed working 2026-11-19 by a live lookup; the board name came from an OSRS Tools post). Normal hiscores do not show Hardcore Worlds progress.
- No XP or drop-rate boosts. Bounty Hunter and LMS were reportedly disabled at launch. Task completion gives raffle entries.
- **Primary sources to pin:** `Update:Hardcore Worlds Are Coming On November 4th`, wiki page `Hardcore Worlds`, and the Jagex launch news post.

## The 12 months before 2026-11-19 (high-level leads)
- **19 Nov 2025: Sailing** released, the first new skill in over a decade. Quests include Pandemonium (unlocks the skill), Prying Times, Current Affairs, Troubled Tortugans and others. ⚠ Snippets disagree on quest XP totals.
- **30 Jun 2026: The Blood Moon Rises** (correction 2026-11-20: an earlier note said Sins of the Father; the methods desk's pinned wiki read shows The Blood Moon Rises released that day; Sins of the Father is a separate, earlier quest required for the Hallowed Sepulchre).
- **Jul 2026: Wyrmscraig** island, with a new quest and The Mad Angel boss.
- **14 Oct 2026: More Gathering Efficiencies** (QoL: tackle-box storage, runite golem changes). Further gathering QoL on 11 Nov (coconuts, drift net banking, law altar). No XP-rate changes reported, which needs confirming.
- **Fractured Archive** (new content; loot scaling changed 11 Nov; practice mode and duo changes 18 Nov).
